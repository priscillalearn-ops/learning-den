【Email・辦公室搬遷】Dear staff, as you know, our office will move to the Grant Building next month. The new space is much larger and will ____ all of our departments on a single floor. || accommodate | accumulate | accuse | acquaint || accommodate (v.) 容納　新辦公室夠大，能把所有部門放在同一層

【備忘錄・資訊安全】To protect company data, all employees are required to change their passwords every 90 days. Passwords must contain at least eight characters and should not be ____ with anyone. || shared | shaped | shifted | shortened || shared (v.) 分享　密碼不能和別人分享
【Email・會議改期】Unfortunately, Mr. Hayes has a scheduling ____ on Thursday, so the budget meeting has been moved to Friday at 10 a.m. || conflict | contract | consent | content || scheduling conflict (phr.) 行程衝突
【公告・志工活動】Our company will hold a beach cleanup on Saturday, April 12. All employees and their families are welcome to ____. || participate | anticipate | precipitate | particular || participate (v.) 參加　句尾不接受詞，用 participate
【Email・付款提醒】This is a friendly reminder that invoice #4471 is now ten days past ____. Please arrange payment at your earliest convenience. || due | dew | dual | duty || past due (phr.) 逾期
【公告・新產品】We are proud to introduce the X5 headphones, our lightest model ever. Thanks to their ____ design, you can wear them comfortably all day. || ergonomic | economic | ecological | energetic || ergonomic (adj.) 符合人體工學的
【Email・旅遊行程】Your flight to Seoul departs at 8:15 a.m. Please arrive at the airport at least two hours before ____ to allow time for check-in and security. || departure | deposit | department | desperation || departure (n.) 出發；起飛

【新聞稿・新店開幕】The new store, ____ seats more than 80 people, also features an outdoor patio. || which | who | where | what || which　非限定關代，當子句主詞修飾 store
【備忘錄・資訊安全】If you receive a suspicious email, do not click any links. ____, forward the message to the IT department immediately. || Instead | Besides | Otherwise | Likewise || Instead 改為；而是　不要點連結，而是轉寄給 IT
【Email・會議改期】Please let me know ____ the new time works for you. || whether | that | what | which || whether 是否　讓我知道新時間是否可以
【公告・志工活動】Lunch and water ____ for all volunteers. Please wear comfortable shoes and bring a hat. || will be provided | will provide | provided | are providing || will be provided　午餐「被提供」，未來被動
【Email・付款提醒】If you ____ already sent the payment, please disregard this message. || have | has | had | having || have already sent　主詞 you 用 have，現在完成式
【公告・新產品】The X5 headphones will be available in stores ____ June 1. || starting | started | to start | starts || starting + 日期　從…開始
【Email・旅遊行程】Your hotel reservation at the Grand Seoul ____ for three nights, from March 3 to March 6. || has been confirmed | confirmed | is confirming | confirms || has been confirmed　訂房「已被確認」
【公告・電梯維修】The work is expected to take most of the day. ____ the elevator is back in operation, we will send an email to all staff. || Once | Despite | During | Because of || Once 一旦　接子句；Despite / During / Because of 接名詞
【Email・會議改期】The meeting will now take place in Room 405 ____ Room 210. || instead of | in spite of | because of | in case of || instead of 而不是　改在 405 而不是 210
